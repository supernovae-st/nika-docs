## Meaning and placement

###  → run → access

The serving speed asked of the route · standard (the route's regular tier) · fast (its faster tier, where the route offers one for the selected model). The one closed vocabulary of this declaration: a route binds each word to its own native control and the engine applies that control exactly, or refuses before inference. A word is never translated into another model, another effort or another route's control, and fast is never served as standard. Omitted, the route keeps its own default tier, which may be either and may stay unknown: omission is not standard. A route's native words (`on` · `off` · a boolean) are not values here. There is no third value.
