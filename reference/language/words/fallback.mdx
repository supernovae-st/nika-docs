## Meaning and placement

###  → run → access

What may replace the declared selection · none, also the meaning of an omitted fallback once access is declared: no other route, protocol, model, effort or service tier, before admission or after it — an unavailable route refuses before inference and a route failing mid-run fails its task, never re-routed. Declared alternatives are unspecified; a later value is additive.
