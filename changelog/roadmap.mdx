Nika lets you put AI workflows in an application, describe their permitted
effects, and inspect what happened after a run. This page separates the parts
you can use from the goals that still require implementation and evidence.

## What does “Compile” mean here?

The released command creates a workflow from a bounded skeleton. It is a useful
starting point you can check, edit and run. Broader natural-language composition,
cross-surface SDK integration and advanced compiler goals need their own release
and qualification evidence. The name alone does not establish those capabilities.

Start with [the available authoring path](/examples/bounded-authoring); follow the
[engine roadmap](https://github.com/supernovae-st/nika/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) and
[public engine issues](https://github.com/supernovae-st/nika/issues) for ongoing work.

## What must be ready before the official launch?

The public roadmap targets semantic versioning toward **1.0**. The native engine
is already available, but that availability is separate from completing the
original launch requirements. The owning roadmap and specification describe:

- **Release and user experience qualification:** installation, documentation,
  editor setup and supported execution paths must work together.
- **The contract system and whole Connectome:** the planned memory and cognition
  system must meet its declared scope and evidence requirements.
- **Long-term compatibility:** conformance, archives, deterministic checking,
  a compatibility promise and succession are launch conditions.
- **Design-partner release candidates:** the public roadmap places these before
  the official launch.

These are conditions, not delivery dates or claims of completion. The
[owning engine roadmap](https://github.com/supernovae-st/nika/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)
and the specification's
[timeline](https://github.com/supernovae-st/nika-spec/blob/main/timeline/timeline.yaml)
carry the detailed decisions. [Timeline and evidence](/reference/timeline)
explains how to read them without confusing a proposal with a shipped feature.
